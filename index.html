MONEY THAT LOGS ITSELF.

Connect your bank messages and Apple Pay taps once, and Hekta logs your spending for you, without opening the app. Ask where your money went, in Arabic or English.

01

Three sources

Connect it once.

Your bank’s messages and your Apple Pay taps become rows in your Feed, without opening the app. And you can always write one yourself.

  • Bank messages

    One Shortcuts automation hands Hekta each message your bank sends. Hekta reads CIB, NBE, QNB and more by fixed rules, and its AI reads the rest.

  • Apple Pay taps

    An in-store tap logs itself, with the merchant and the amount. Online purchases still arrive from your bank’s message.

  • You

    Write what you spent in the bar, paste a message, share or photograph a receipt, or tell Siri “Log an expense with Hekta”.

A row read by fixed rules, from a bank Hekta knows or an Apple Pay tap, saves on its own, and every one can be undone. Anything the AI reads waits for you to confirm it. Any source can be switched to “Ask me first”.

02

One Feed, one bar

What you have, and what’s already spoken for.

Your balance and what’s left to spend share one card at the top of the Feed, with a bar that shows your pace through the month. Below it is everything that happened, day by day.

  • Ask

    One bar, two jobs. Ask where your money went, what’s due, who owes you or what you’re worth, or write what you spent. Nothing you write is saved until you confirm it.

  • Free answers

    The common questions are answered from your own data straight away and do not use your AI allowance.

  • Budgets

    Weekly, monthly or yearly, per category or across everything, with a pace line that says whether you are ahead of the calendar.

  • What’s coming

    Subscriptions and instalment plans, and what’s due next.

  • Everything

    Accounts, transfers and categories. Every list is in Everything, behind the icon at the top of the Feed.

  • Recaps & widgets

    A recap of your week each Sunday and your year each December. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Control Center control.

03

The Vault

Everything you own, priced today.

Assets are valued on the server from live feeds, in your base currency. When a price cannot be fetched, Hekta shows a dash rather than a zero.

  • Gold

    By weight and karat — 24 down to 10K, never ounces. The way you actually buy it.

  • Stocks & crypto

    Holdings priced live, each with its own subtotal and the date it was last updated.

  • Cash

    Your accounts join the same total, so net worth is one number and not a spreadsheet.

04

People

What you split, settled.

A trip, a flat, a family. Hekta tracks who paid and works out who owes whom. Groups are free, with no limit.

  • Four ways to split

    Equally, by shares, by exact amounts, or by percentage.

  • Settle up

    Balances per person and per currency, and a settle-up to clear them.

  • Lending

    A private record of what you lent and borrowed, person by person. Share it with another Hekta user when you both agree.

  • Family

    A shared household view for up to 5 people. Visibility is off until you turn it on.

Privacy

Private by design.

  • No ads

    No cross-app tracking. No third-party analytics. We never sell your data.

  • Stored in the EU

    Your financial data is stored in the EU and protected so that only you can read it.

  • Nothing kept

    Receipt photos are deleted once the transaction is saved. Hekta never stores your voice.

  • Locked to you

    App Lock asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode every time Hekta opens.

  • Yours to take

    Export your transactions to CSV for free, and delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.
