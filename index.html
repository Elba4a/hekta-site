01

The AI input layer

Skip the forms.
Just say it.

Type it, speak it, or photograph it. Hekta turns it into a clean record you confirm.

  • Text

    “spent 50 on coffee at Starbucks” → a categorized transaction.

  • Voice

    Tap the mic and say it, logged in seconds.

  • Receipt & SMS

    Photograph a paper receipt or a bank SMS screenshot and Hekta reads the amount, merchant, and date.

  • Or just ask

    “How much did I spend on restaurants in March?”

Nothing is ever saved until you tap confirm — the assistant only proposes.

02

Your day-to-day

What you have, and what’s already spoken for.

One card, two figures: the money in your accounts, and what is genuinely left once your budgets, subscriptions and instalments are counted.

  • Accounts

    Every account in its own currency, and one balance across all of them.

  • Budgets

    Weekly, monthly or yearly, per category or across everything, with a pace line that says whether you are ahead of the calendar.

  • What’s coming

    Subscriptions and instalment plans, due before they surprise you.

  • Analytics

    Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, and trends. Plus a recap of your week each Sunday, and your year each December.

  • Search

    One field for transactions, people, and every screen in the app.

  • Widgets

    Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and Control Center controls.

03

The Vault

Everything you own, priced today.

Assets are valued on the server from live feeds, in your base currency. When a price cannot be fetched, Hekta shows a dash rather than a zero.

  • Gold

    By weight and karat — 24 down to 10K, never ounces. The way you actually buy it.

  • Stocks & crypto

    Holdings priced live, each with its own subtotal and the date it was last updated.

  • Cash

    Your accounts join the same total, so net worth is one number and not a spreadsheet.

04

People

What you split, settled.

A trip, a flat, a family. Hekta tracks who paid, works out who owes whom, and suggests the fewest transfers that clear it.

  • Four ways to split

    Equally, by shares, by exact amounts, or by percentage.

  • Split a receipt

    Scan it, check the lines, tap who had what, and settle up.

  • Settle up

    Balances per person, per currency, with suggested settlements.

  • Lending

    Money lent to one person, tracked on its own, without a group.

  • Family

    A shared household view for up to 5 people. Visibility is off until you turn it on.

Multi-currency

Last year’s numbers stay last year’s numbers.

Every entry is locked to its exchange rate on the day you enter it. Rates move; your record does not. Watch the middle column.

Pair Rate that day Today Your record

SAR → AED 0.979333 0.979333 AED 489.67

Illustrative figures, measured on one day in August 2026 — not a live quote. Hekta prices from live feeds inside the app, and a record written at a rate keeps that rate whatever the market does next.

  • Gold by karat

    24 down to 10K, by weight — never ounces. Priced against the day’s spot, in your base currency.

  • Arabic & English

    Fully bilingual with native right-to-left layout, designed in rather than bolted on. Western numerals in both, because that is how the region reads money.

Privacy

Private by design.

  • No ads

    No cross-app tracking. No third-party analytics. We never sell your data.

  • Stored in the EU

    Your financial data is stored in the EU and protected so that only you can read it.

  • Nothing kept

    Receipts are processed once and then deleted; your voice recordings are never stored by us.

  • Locked to you

    App Lock asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode every time Hekta opens.

  • Yours to take

    Export your data to CSV anytime, and delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.
